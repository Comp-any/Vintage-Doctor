== Where the server's time goes ==
• Nothing measured yet.
  The game can time every part of a tick: creatures, each kind of machine and block that does something, each mod's own work. The doctor switches that on, adds it up, and switches it off again. Each side measures itself: the button on your game's half times your game's frames, the one on the server's half the server's ticks. It runs for that many seconds of real time, so a game that lags fits fewer ticks into it, and the slow ones show as the time they took: that is what it is for. The window counts down while it runs.
